# MINIMAL THESIS ANALYSIS PLAN

## Purpose

Define only the analyses required to support Thesis Parts I–II after the sample × analysis inventory has been reconstructed.

## Planning sequence

1. Establish cohort composition and modality availability.
2. Inventory completed pipelines/analyses at sample level.
3. Identify the major biological questions for Thesis Part II.
4. Map each question to existing results.
5. Identify the smallest set of unresolved questions that require new analysis.
6. Execute and QC only those analyses.
7. Build the thesis figures/tables.
8. Defer publication-expansion analyses until after thesis submission unless an analysis is necessary for scientific validity.

## Candidate questions to evaluate

- What is the composition and modality coverage of the cohort?
- What antigen classes are represented in the discovery space?
- What is the yield/coverage of the integrated platform across samples and tumor types?
- How do candidate antigen characteristics differ across tumor types or data modalities?
- Which results demonstrate the value of integrating complementary omics modalities?
- Which representative antigen classes/examples best support the chapter narrative?

These are planning prompts, not assumed results. The final questions must follow from the actual data and platform outputs.
